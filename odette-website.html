My Story

My journey has been one of resilience, reinvention, and proven results. After suffering a career-threatening ankle injury, I pioneered my own rehabilitation methods, defying expectations to return stronger than ever.

🏆 In 2024: I won three national titles and claimed the World Champion Masters 70m Beach Sprint title—proof that intelligent training, scientific expertise, and mindset mastery transform lives.

My experiences competing on Survivor Australia and running in Death Valley have shaped my deep understanding of endurance, adaptability, and mental toughness. These aren't just achievements—they're lessons I share with every client.

With a Bachelor of Health Science and Master's in Podiatric Medicine, I've worked with global brands including Runner's World, Lorna Jane, Under Armour, and True Protein. I was recognized as Most Innovative Trainer by Sir Richard Branson's Roger Magazine.

Today, I apply this unique blend of clinical expertise, lived experience, and practical insight to help everyone from elite athletes to corporate teams unlock their potential. Whether you're recovering from injury, optimizing performance, or building lasting health, I bring evidence-based strategies rooted in real-world application.
